Privacy and terms

Dustpan collects nothing.

No account, no analytics, no crash reports. Here's exactly what the app and this page do with your data.

The app

  • Everything runs on your Mac. Scan results stay in memory and disappear when you quit.
  • Dustpan writes two files of its own: a history of what it moved to the Trash and what you asked it to ignore (~/Library/Application Support/Dustpan/), and, only after you open the Updates tab, a cached copy of Homebrew's public app list (about 1 MB).
  • It looks at file names, sizes and dates. It reads what's inside a file only to work out a fingerprint when you ask it to find duplicates, and that fingerprint never leaves your Mac.
  • It connects to the internet only when you open the Updates tab in Apps. Then it asks the Mac App Store, Homebrew and each app's own update feed whether newer versions exist. It sends app identifiers and your store region, never your files. Those servers see your IP address, just as they do when your apps check for updates themselves.

This website

This page sets no cookies and loads no trackers, analytics or fonts from anyone else. Like any web server, the host that serves these pages may keep standard access logs (IP address, time, page requested, browser) for security and to keep the site running. Dustpan's maker doesn't use those logs to identify anyone.
